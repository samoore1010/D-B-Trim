About D&B

An established finish carpentry contractor

For approximately 25 years, D&B has been a dependable finish carpentry partner to contractors, developers, and owners across Michigan.

01 / Our story

D&B Trim Carpentry is a finish carpentry contractor built around experience, reliability, and long-term relationships.

The company has spent roughly 25 years installing architectural finish materials on construction projects throughout Michigan. Much of that experience has been in commercial, multifamily, and production construction, where consistency, coordination, and manpower matter as much as the finished product.

D&B has grown primarily through repeat business and referrals from contractors and partners who know what to expect when the company is on the job. As the business continues to grow, our capabilities extend well beyond any single market—positioning D&B as a finish carpentry contractor for a wide range of construction opportunities.
