00 / Who we are

D&B Trim Carpentry is a finish carpentry contractor specializing in the installation of architectural finish materials.

From commercial door and hardware packages to trim, cabinetry, and architectural millwork, our crews provide the skilled installation, coordination, and manpower that bring projects to completion. For approximately 25 years, D&B has worked alongside general contractors, construction managers, developers, and owners as a dependable finish carpentry partner—much of that experience in commercial, multifamily, and production construction, with capabilities that extend across a wide range of project types.

01 Door, Frame & Hardware Installation Professional installation of interior and exterior doors, hollow metal and wood frames, commercial door hardware, pocket door systems, and finish hardware—for projects ranging from custom homes to large-scale commercial developments.
